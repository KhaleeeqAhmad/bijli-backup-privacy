Bijli Backup – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · Developer: Khaleeq Ahmad · Contact: khaleeqahmad2022@gmail.com

Bijli Backup is an offline battery-backup runtime calculator. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

Bijli Backup does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Your battery settings, the appliances you add, and any setups you save.
  • Your preferences: language, light or dark theme, and whether you have finished the first-run screens.

This information stays only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party. The app does not request any permissions, and automatic cloud backup of the app's data is turned off.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account details.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code, and it does not connect to the internet.

Deleting your data

You can reset saved data inside the app under Settings, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Accuracy of results

Runtime figures are estimates based on the values you enter. Real battery performance varies with age, temperature and equipment. Do not rely on the app for safety-critical decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: khaleeqahmad2022@gmail.com
